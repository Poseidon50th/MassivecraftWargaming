      Condensed field guide

      How the battle resolves

      1. Orders: choose three distinct units, or every remaining unit if fewer than three survive.
      2. Movement: paths are orthogonal and may turn. Enemy-occupied squares block movement.
      3. Collision: higher Initiative takes a shared destination; ties send both units back.
      4. Control: compare the sum projected by each army on every square.
      5. Casualties: a unit ending on enemy-controlled ground is destroyed.
      6. Cavalry: projects 4 control onto an enemy without adjacent allies. If the opposing control needed to destroy Cavalry comes only from units also marked for destruction, the Cavalry survives.

      Rotation consumes an order and does not count as a safe move. Artillery may rotate but cannot move.
